1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how GenieACS Mobile Client ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Alijaya Network, handles information when you use our mobile application to connect to your TR-069 CWMP / GenieACS server.

Privacy Commitment: GenieACS Mobile Client is a direct client management tool. We do NOT collect, sell, or monetize your network data. All TR-069 communication occurs directly between your mobile device and your configured GenieACS NBI server.

2. Data We Process & Permissions

A. Server Connection & Credentials

To communicate with your GenieACS server via port 7557 NBI, you provide your server URL, username, and password. This credential information is stored locally and securely on your device using Android private app storage. It is never transmitted to any third-party server.

B. Location Data (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

  • Purpose: Location permissions are requested exclusively when you open the Network Map (Peta Jaringan). It enables field technicians to view their real-time GPS position relative to ODP (Optical Distribution Points) and client ONUs, and to launch route navigation via Google Maps.
  • Storage: Real-time user GPS coordinates are processed on your device only and are NOT uploaded or stored in any remote tracking database.

C. Audio & Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO)

  • Purpose: Audio permission is required only if you activate the optional Voice Assistant feature.
  • Usage: Speech input is converted to text on-device to query your modem status or ask troubleshooting questions. Audio recordings are never retained or archived.

D. Network Data (INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)

Used to send HTTP/HTTPS REST API requests to your GenieACS NBI server to retrieve and update modem parameters (e.g. WiFi SSID/Password, Optical Rx/Tx Power, PPPoE WAN status, and Reboot commands).

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google Gemini AI (Optional): If you configure a Gemini API key for the voice assistant, text queries are processed according to Google Cloud and Gemini API privacy terms.
  • OpenStreetMap / Leaflet: Map tile layers are rendered from public OpenStreetMap servers. No personal data is shared with tile providers.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Since all credentials, cached device info, and user preferences reside solely on your device, you have complete control over your data. You may delete all stored data at any time by:

  • Logging out from the app menu (Clears session and credentials).
  • Clearing the app storage in Android Settings > Apps > GenieACS > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the application.

5. Children's Privacy

GenieACS Mobile Client is a technical network management utility intended for ISP administrators and field technicians. It is not directed at or intended for children under 13 years of age.
